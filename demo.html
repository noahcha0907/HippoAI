HIPPO / A REAL EEG EXPERIMENT

Can AI repair
another AI?

We gave a small brain-signal model a misleading blink cue. Then we asked Astra to reduce its reliance on that shortcut with tiny weight edits.

Real saved results. No training runs during the demo.
THE RESULT

A promising first step.

Loading the recorded results...

Passing means better accuracy when the cue changes, fewer artifact-driven prediction changes, and preservation checks passed.

01 / THE PROBLEM

A misleading cue.

A blink pattern was linked to a label during training. We changed that link to test whether the model relied on it.

02 / THE EDIT

Change a few weights.

Astra used the weights and development-test feedback to propose edits. Each candidate was tested on a copy.

03 / THE CHECK

Keep useful behavior.

We compared the selected edits with the unchanged model and simple controls on a separate recording session.

Early evidence from one person and three training seeds. The final session had been inspected before this follow-up; broader validation is next.
