Hippo

Surgical repair
for clinical AI.

One failure can undermine an otherwise useful model. Hippo explores how to locate the weights behind that failure, make a targeted edit, and test what changed.

Our proposed pipeline connects model auditing with independent validation and a report clinicians can understand. The goal is to make specific repairs without a full retrain.

Accuracy can give way to agreement.

3parameters

A small edit can make a difference.

A radiotherapy-network study located and repaired 16 of 18 planted flaws, changing a median of three parameters per repair.

Nusrat & Nusrat, 2026 ↗
0.016% of weights

Targeted repair has a proof point.

A Qwen3.5-0.8B edit reduced reasoning loops and raised MATH-500 accuracy from 7.9% to 48.1%. This was a non-clinical experiment.

Takehi et al., 2026 ↗

Explore the workflow ↗

Audit

Investigate which weights may be driving a known failure.

Repair

Propose a small edit and record exactly what changes.

Validate

Use independent tests to check the fix and look for regressions.

Report

Explain the errors, their potential impact, and the risk that remains.

Count the errors.
Understand the harm.

A missed cancer and a missed mild inflammation may both count as one false negative. Their consequences can be very different.

Hippo’s reporting approach is informed by research that connects technical errors to pathology and clinical impact. That context matters as much as the score.

Evans & Snead, 2024 ↗
